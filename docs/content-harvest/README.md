## Headline findings

- **The public site is small.** Its visible menu is Home, Church, About, Connect, Music.
  Most of its 30 pages are hidden forms, dated campaigns or Devo Bible videos.
- **Core content worth keeping:** mission statement, founding story and lead pastors,
  the 19-point Declaration of Faith, two worship locations with times, Christlike Worship
  and Radical Music descriptions, social/Spotify/YouTube destinations, logos.
- **Facts conflict** in several places (Scarborough unit number, youth convention
  address, desktop vs mobile Declaration of Faith) → see REVIEW-NEEDED.
- **No sermon archive, events calendar, ministry descriptions, staff bios, email address
  or giving information** exist on the old site. The new Sermons, Ministries and
  New Here pages will need new content from the church.
- **Videos (~1.4 GB)** were inventoried, not downloaded; storage/hosting is a decision.
