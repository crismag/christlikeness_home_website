PRAYERS FOR DELIVERANCE

Deliverance from the past.

Father, in Jesus’s name, I loose myself from this prowling demon that sought to steal, kill, and destroy my body, my sexuality, and my worth.

I loose myself from the shame, guilt, soul ties, and any other hindering spirit that would try to keep me from living a whole and healthy life.

I loose myself from every demonic spirit that would seek to enter through this open door, and I shut this door to my past and pray a hedge of protection around my future.

I loose myself from the effects of all the bad memories, painful memories, and memories of the past that would hinder me in the present or future. Amen.

Deliverance from ungodly ties.

Father, in Jesus’s name, I loose myself from all relationships that are not ordained of God, all relationships that are not of the Spirit but of the flesh, all relationship based on control, domination, or manipulation, and all relationships based on lust and deception.

Deliverance from unforgiveness and bitterness.

Father, in Jesus’s name, I loose myself from all bitterness, unforgiveness, and resentment.

I loose to God those who have offended me or hurt me in anyway.

I loose myself from all spirits of infirmity as a result of my bitterness.

I close that door, in Jesus’s name. Amen.

Deliverance from emotions.

In the name of the Lord Jesus Christ, by the authority given to me to bind and loose, I loose my emotions from every evil spirit that has come in as a result of my experiences.

I loose myself from all hurt, deep hurt, pain, sadness, grief, anger, hatred, rage, bitterness, fear, and bound and blocked emotions.

I command these spirits to come out, and I decree freedom to my emotions in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ. Amen.

Deliverance from occult bondage.

Father, in Jesus’s name, I loose myself from all occult involvement, all sorcery, divination, witchcraft, psychic inheritance, rebellion, all confusion, sickness, death, and destruction as a result of occult involvement. Amen.

Deliverance from mind control.

In Jesus’s name, I loose my mind from all spirits of control, confusion, mental bondage, insanity, madness, fantasy, passivity, intellectualism, knowledge block, ignorance, mind binding, lust, and evil thinking. Amen

Deliverance from manipulated will.

Father, in Jesus’s name, I loose my will from all control, domination, and manipulation from Satan, his demons, and other people.

I loose my will from all lust, rebellion, stubbornness, pride, self-will, selfishness, and anti-submissive spirits that block and hinder my will.

I break and loose myself from all chains around my will, and I submit my will to the will of God. Amen.

Deliverance from lust.

In the name of Jesus, I loose all members of my body— including my mind, memory, eyes, ears, tongue, hands, feet, and my entire sexual character—from all lust, perversion, sexual impurity, uncleanness, lasciviousness, promiscuity, pornography, fornication, homosexuality, fantasy, filthiness, burning passion, and uncontrollable sex drive. Amen.

Deliverance from evil inheritance.

In the name of Jesus, I loose myself from all evil inheritance, including inherited weaknesses, attitudes, thought patterns, sickness, witchcraft, lust, rebellion, poverty, ungodly lifestyles, and strife. Amen.

Deliverance from fear.

In the name of Jesus, I loose myself from all fears including childhood fears, fears from trauma, fears from the past, and all inherited fears. Amen.

Deliverance from rejection.

In the name of Jesus, I loose myself from the spirit of rejection.

I loose myself from self- rejection and sabotage. I loose myself from fear of man and people-pleasing. I seek only to please God.

I loose myself to receive love from God and from others without fear.

I close the door on rejection, fear, hurt, unforgiveness, bitterness, and rebellion.

In Jesus’s name, I pray. Amen.

Deliverance from condemnation.

In the name of Jesus, I loose myself from all guilt, shame, condemnation, self- condemnation, and legalism. Amen.

DECLARATION PRAYERS

Father, I thank you for the power of your Word. Your Word declares in Psalm 16:11 You have made known to me the path of life; You will fill me with joy in Your presence, with eternal pleasures at Your right hand.

I shall decree a thing, and it shall be established in my life. (Job 22:28)

It is You who arms me with strength and makes my way perfect. (Psalm 18:32)

The weapons of my warfare are not carnal but mighty through God to the pulling down of strongholds. (2 Corinthians 10:4)

I command all generational curse that came into my life during conception, in my father’s bloodline and in my mother’s bloodline to come out in the name of Jesus. I command all generational spirits of sickness, infirmity, disease, failure, and poverty to come out of my life in the name of Jesus. (Exodus 34:7)

I break all ungodly covenants and renew my covenant to God through the body and blood of Jesus. I am joined to the Lord, and I am one spirit with Him. (1 Corinthians 6:17)

Lord, surround me with Your shield of protection. (Psalm 5:12)

I will be satisfied with long life, and God will show me His salvation. (Psalm 91:16)

Lord, bless my latter end more than my beginning. (Job 42:12)

Let shalom (peace) be within my walls. (Psalm 122:7)

Let Your wisdom be a tree of life to me. (Proverbs 3:18)

Lord, give me Your favor, for in Your favor is life. (Psalm. 30:5)

Let the power in Your hands be released in my life. (Habakkuk 3:4)

Let me stand perfect and complete in all the will of God. (Colossians 4:12)

As I pay attention to the commands of the LORD my God that He gave me this day and carefully follow them. I will always be at the top, never at the bottom. (Deuteronomy 28:13)

Let my whole spirit, soul and body be preserved blameless unto the coming of my Lord Jesus Christ (1 Thessalonians 5:23)

I receive all these things through the apostolic anointing. IN CHRIST JESUS’ NAME, AMEN!
